Frequently asked questions

What kind of brands do you work with?

I’ve worked across sports media, tech start-ups, lifestyle brands and iGaming. My content and social strategies adapt to whatever the audience cares about, whether it’s Premier League transfers, salon bookings, or price-comparison tools.

What does "built with AI" mean?

I use Claude and ChatGPT to build functional apps, dashboards and workflows. No coding background — just a clear brief and fast iteration. I’ve shipped a Mac menubar app, a performance dashboard and automated reporting templates this way.

Are you available for freelance work?

Yes — I’m open to both full-time roles and freelance projects. I work remotely from India and am comfortable across time zones. Drop me a line at sripad@falsewinger.com or use the contact form.

What’s your turnaround on content projects?

It depends on scope, but I ran all 104 World Cup matches worth of content from planning through design to publishing for every matchday. I move fast, especially on live events and news-led content.

Can you manage a writing team?

Absolutely. At Sportskeeda, I managed a pool of freelance writers, and at ViralFooty I hired and trained a team of eight in writing, graphics and publishing. I’m comfortable managing contributors and setting editorial standards.
