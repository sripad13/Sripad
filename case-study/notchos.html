Features

What it does

Each piece lives in the notch, so it’s always one hover away. Here is what each one is for.

15:00

Pomodoro timer

Focus and break sessions with the border as the progress bar. It runs down, red for focus and green for break.

  • Repeat rounds, auto-start, skip and restart
  • Daily streak with a week view
  • Floating pill that you can resize from its edges

Why I added it: Long pieces and match reports need blocks of uninterrupted focus. The timer keeps those blocks to a set length, and the streak gives a daily goal to hit.

Now playing

Music in the notch

Play, pause, skip and scrub for Apple Music, Spotify and browser audio.

  • Album art and a level meter in the closed notch
  • YouTube titles and thumbnails in the browser
  • 10-second skip and progress for supported apps

Why I added it: I listen to music or videos while I write and edit. Controlling playback from the notch means I don’t have to switch windows and break my flow.

Battery alerts

A popup when the battery is low or full, with a faint corner glow that fades when you dismiss it.

  • Set your own low and full levels
  • Under the notch or centred on the screen
  • Works on external displays too

Why I added it: I often write away from the charger. A warning gives me time to save my work and plug in before the laptop dies in the middle of a draft.

Messages from your phone, pasted in one place

Messages with your phone

Connected through a Telegram bot of your own. Send text from the Mac to your phone, and read replies in the notch.

  • Copy any message with one click
  • Save notes and a clipboard history
  • /remind for timed reminders with a popup

Why I added it: I write on the Mac and check my phone on the move. Sending text between them means ideas and quotes don’t get lost, and reminders keep deadlines and post times in view.

58% · Start speed test

Battery and speed

Battery percentage with time left, next to a one-tap internet speed test.

Why I added it: Uploads and live streams fail on a weak connection. A quick check before I start tells me whether to go ahead or wait.

Matte or Liquid Glass

Two looks

A flat matte style that blends into the notch, or a frosted Liquid Glass style. One switch in Settings.

Why I added it: The notch sits in the middle of my screen every day, so it should look calm and not distract. Two styles let me pick the one that suits the day.

Under the hood

How it’s built

Written in Swift for macOS, with a single codebase of about 3,500 lines.

SwiftUI + AppKitCustom panels, the notch window and the floating timer
Core AudioDetects which app is making sound
AppleScriptReads and controls Music, Spotify and browser tabs
Telegram Bot APITwo-way messages, with the owner locked to one chat
Local storageSettings, notes and streaks stay on the Mac
AccessibilityMedia keys for apps that need them, with a clear repair step

Built solo as a personal project to learn macOS app development, then designed around how people actually use a Mac day to day. It isn’t sold or distributed.
