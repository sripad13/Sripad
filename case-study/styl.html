Case study

Building the content engine for a salon-booking app

STYL was a salon-booking app that needed to be found, and to keep its users coming back. I covered both: search content to bring people in, and CRM, email and app messaging to bring them back.

100+SEO articles written
4Channels: search, email, CRM and app
Project details

The brief

ClientSTYL
My roleData & Content Strategist
When2015
SectorLifestyle & beauty tech

STYL was a salon-booking app entering a crowded market. It needed organic visibility to acquire users and a retention plan to keep them coming back after the first booking.

What I did

Process

1

Built the search footprint

Researched keywords around salon services, beauty tips and local booking queries, then wrote and published SEO articles to build STYL's organic presence from scratch.

2

Wrote the retention funnel

Created email sequences, CRM messaging and in-app notifications to bring users back after their first booking.

3

Measured and adjusted

Tracked which content drove sign-ups and repeat bookings, then shifted effort toward what was working.

4

Kept teams in step

Coordinated with product, design and marketing so the content plan stayed aligned with feature launches and campaigns.

5

Researched the market

Analysed competitor positioning and customer feedback to find the angles STYL could own in its content.

Outcomes

Results

  • A library of 100+ search articles behind the brand.
  • Retention messaging across email, CRM and in-app notifications.
  • A direct line from customer feedback into the content plan.
