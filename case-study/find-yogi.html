Case study · FindYogi

Growing a tech start-up's organic traffic 3,000% in three months

FindYogi needed people to find it through search. I turned the company blog and articles on other sites into its main growth channel.

3,000%Organic traffic growth
3Months to get there
0Followers when the tech social accounts started

The brief

FindYogi, a tech start-up, needed more people to find it through search without paying for every visit, plus social accounts to support the brand.

What I did

  1. Grew the company blog through search

    Planned and wrote blog content aimed at what people were searching for.

  2. Placed articles on other sites

    Published external articles that pointed readers and search engines back to FindYogi.

  3. Wrote the funnel copy

    Wrote the copy that moved visitors from article to product.

  4. Revised by the numbers

    Tracked performance data and rewrote content that wasn’t pulling its weight.

  5. Launched the tech social accounts

    Created tech-focused social posts and grew the accounts from scratch.

Results

  • Organic website traffic up 3,000% in three months.
  • Brand social accounts built from zero.

Measurement note: Baseline and final traffic numbers behind the 3,000% figure are not published here.
