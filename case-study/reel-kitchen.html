What it does

A link goes in, a structured recipe comes out, and the app keeps it for later.

A recipe from a link

Paste a reel link and the video is watched for you: the voiceover, the on-screen text and every step.

  • Ingredients with quantities and units
  • Steps in order, with timings where there’s waiting
  • Course, cuisine, difficulty and tags

How it works: The vidIQ connector writes down what the video shows and says. Claude then turns those notes into a recipe in a fixed structure.

When the link can’t be watched

For a private account or a photo post, paste the caption or transcript, or add screenshots of the ingredient list. Anything added is combined with what the video shows.

Guesses are labelled

Short videos often skip quantities. Where one is missing, a typical amount is filled in and marked “est.” so you know it didn’t come from the video.

What’s in your kitchen

Add the ingredients you have and the recipes re-rank by how much of each you can already make.

  • Each card shows what you have and what’s missing
  • Salt, oil and water are assumed

A menu board

Saved recipes are grouped by course and can be filtered by tag. Inside a recipe, changing the servings rescales every quantity.

Step 2 of 5One step per screen, with a timer

Cook mode

Cook mode shows one step at a time in large type, with a progress bar across the top.

  • A timer on any step that needs one
  • A beep when the timer finishes
  • Large back and next buttons

Example recipes fill the board until the first reel is added, so the app is useful to look at from the first visit.
